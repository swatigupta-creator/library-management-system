Gautam Buddha Central Library

Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University's central library serves students, research scholars, and faculty with comprehensive print collections, digital repositories, air-conditioned reading halls, and automated circulation services.

📍 Location: Vidya Vihar, Lucknow
🕒 Hours: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM
📚 Collection: 50,000+ Volumes
🌐 Facilities: Wi-Fi & E-Resource Lab

Key System Highlights

🎓 Student Portal

Students can create an account, log in, browse the database catalog, borrow available books, and track their borrowed books and due dates.

🛡️ Librarian Administration

Librarians can add, edit, and delete books, register members, issue books, and process returns with automatic inventory restoration.

⚡ Real-time Availability

When a book is borrowed or issued, available copy count decreases. When returned, available count increments immediately.
